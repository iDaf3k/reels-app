Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Reels ("the app") is a self-hosted application that runs on the user's own computer. This policy explains how it handles data when you connect a Google account.

Data we access

When you authorize the app with Google, it requests permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel and, where enabled, to read basic channel and video statistics. The app only accesses data needed to perform those actions.

How data is used

OAuth tokens and video metadata are used solely to upload and schedule videos you choose and to show you performance statistics within the app. Data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties.

Where data is stored

All tokens, settings and media remain on your own computer. The app operator runs no servers that receive or store your Google user data.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete local data by removing the app's data folder.
